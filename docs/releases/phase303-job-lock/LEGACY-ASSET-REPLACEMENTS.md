# Legacy sample asset replacements

The retired sample image files were unavailable in the workspace, so these are newly generated replacement artworks, not recovered copies or reconstructions of the former images. They restore the legacy URLs still used by saved drafts and covered by `tests/sample-refresh.test.mjs`.

| Legacy URL | Retired sample | Replacement portrait |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `/images/hero-adventurer.webp` | Luna Nyx | Blonde elf-like adventurer in warm sunset light over a stone city |
| `/images/kael-adventurer.webp` | Kai Valen | Dark-haired swordsman in a rain-darkened harbor at blue hour |
| `/images/seraphine-adventurer.webp` | Seraphine Noir | White-haired, cat-eared healer in a bright crystalline garden |

Each portrait was generated as original high-fantasy MMORPG-inspired character art with the built-in ImageGen tool, then encoded as 1024 × 1536 WebP at quality 88. The prompts asked for a single chest-up character and a distinct atmospheric background, with no card frame, text, logos, watermark, or interface elements. These are sample portrait assets; they do not depict job icons or any existing game character.

Prompt set:

- A golden-blonde, pointed-ear female adventurer in dark leather and bronze travel armor, looking toward sunset over a distant old stone city.
- A thoughtful, black-haired male swordsman in charcoal and steel armor with burgundy cloth, standing against a rain-darkened harbor at blue hour.
- A silver-white-haired, cat-eared female healer in ivory robes with teal details, in a serene crystalline garden sanctuary.
